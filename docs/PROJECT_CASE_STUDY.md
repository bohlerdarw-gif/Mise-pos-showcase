# MISE POS — Project Case Study

## Overview

MISE POS is an independent restaurant-management product project created to explore how restaurants can manage everyday operations from one connected system instead of relying on disconnected tools and manual processes.

## The Problem

Restaurant teams need to coordinate tables, orders, staff, kitchen status, payments, stock, reservations and reporting while service is happening. The challenge is to keep those workflows connected without making the product difficult to use.

## The Product Direction

MISE POS is designed as a role-based restaurant operations platform. Owners, managers, cashiers, waiters, kitchen staff and inventory staff work with the same operational system through different workflows suited to their responsibilities.

## Product Areas

The project includes work around:

- Authentication and role-based access
- Multi-tenant and multi-branch structure
- Menu and product management
- Tables and orders
- Staff and shifts
- Payments and cash movement
- Inventory, recipes and suppliers
- Reports
- Reservations and waitlists
- Customer, loyalty and campaign workflows
- QR workflows
- Printer and kitchen-ticket workflows
- Real-time updates
- Restaurant settings

## Technical Structure

The application uses a monorepo with:

- **Frontend:** Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, TanStack React Query, Zustand and Socket.IO Client
- **Backend:** NestJS, TypeScript, Prisma, PostgreSQL, JWT/Passport and Socket.IO
- **Development:** Node.js workspaces, Git/GitHub and Jest testing setup
